BeyWatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

About BeyWatch

BeyWatch is a WhatsApp-based marketplace alert bot for a private Beyblade community. It helps users find marketplace listings for items they are interested in.

Information We Process

BeyWatch may process WhatsApp user identifiers, message content, images attached to marketplace posts, watchlist preferences, and marketplace listing information.

How We Use Information

This information is used only to identify marketplace listings, match them against user watchlists, and send relevant alerts.

Payments and Purchases

BeyWatch does not automatically purchase products, place bids, or process payments.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

Marketplace listing and watchlist information may be retained while required to provide the service.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the BeyWatch administrator.
