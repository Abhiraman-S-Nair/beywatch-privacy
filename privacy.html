Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

BeyWatch is a community marketplace-alert service designed to help Beyblade collectors discover and track relevant marketplace listings through WhatsApp.

Information We Process

BeyWatch may process WhatsApp user identifiers, marketplace message content, images attached to marketplace posts, watchlist preferences, listing information, and alert records.

How We Use Information

Information is processed to identify marketplace listings, match them against user watchlists, maintain active listing records, and send relevant alerts.

Marketplace Media

Images attached to marketplace messages may be processed when image-based identification is required. Such images may be temporarily or persistently stored by the service depending on the deployment configuration.

Payments and Purchases

BeyWatch does not automatically purchase products, place bids, process payments, or contact sellers on a user's behalf.

Data Sharing

BeyWatch does not sell personal information to third parties. External service providers may process information when required to provide functionality, such as the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API or image-analysis services.

Data Retention

The current implementation may retain marketplace listing data, message text, identifiers, alert records, and media while needed by the service. The current prototype does not yet provide configurable retention periods or user-facing deletion controls.

Security

BeyWatch uses environment-based secrets and validates Meta webhook signatures. Production deployments should additionally use appropriate access controls, secure hosting, data retention policies, and protection for stored media and databases.

Contact

For questions about BeyWatch or this privacy policy, contact the BeyWatch project administrator.

Independent Project

BeyWatch is an independent community project and is not affiliated with Meta, WhatsApp, or Hasbro.
