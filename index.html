🤖 BeyWatch

WhatsApp Marketplace Alerts for Beyblade Collectors

BeyWatch is a community marketplace-alert service designed to help Beyblade collectors discover listings for items they are looking for.

What BeyWatch Does

  • Monitors marketplace messages shared with the service
  • Identifies Beyblade products and accessories
  • Matches listings against user watchlists
  • Sends relevant alerts to interested users

What BeyWatch Does Not Do

  • No automatic purchases
  • No automatic bidding
  • No payment processing

Privacy

BeyWatch processes information only to provide its marketplace alert functionality.
