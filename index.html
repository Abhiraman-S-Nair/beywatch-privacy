How it works

A marketplace post becomes a private notification in a few simple steps.

01 · Post A seller shares a marketplace listing.
02 · Identify BeyWatch analyzes text and, when needed, images.
03 · Match The listing is compared against active watchlists.
04 · Alert Interested collectors receive a private alert.
Historical matching: active listings are retained, so starting a watch later can still surface an existing offer.
